Recognition preview

Your camera feed, the detected hand skeleton, and what the recognizer makes of it — running the same trained model as the desktop app.

This does not control your computer. It is a preview of the recognizer only. Nothing on this page can move your cursor, press a key, or change your volume — browsers do not grant web pages that access. The panel on the right shows which mode would be active if the desktop app were running. Video is processed on your device and is never uploaded.

The demo needs access to your camera.

Gestures to try

Do thisRecognized asDesktop app would
Point your index finger up, thumb tucked Cursor mode Move the pointer
Touch thumb to index fingertip, hold briefly, release Left click Click
Touch middle fingertip to index fingertip, hold, release Right click Right click
Closed fist, then move your hand up or down Scroll Scroll the active window
Thumb up, index down, move up or down Volume Change system volume
L-shape, open palm, or two fingers A trained pose Fire the bound shortcut

The three trained poses are the ones in the shipped model. The model was trained on one person's hands in one room, so it may read your hands less reliably than it reads the author's — retraining on your own samples is described in the guide.

Hand landmarks by MediaPipe Tasks, running locally via WebAssembly.
